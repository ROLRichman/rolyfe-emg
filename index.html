Starter lesson: Ancient Egypt and Nubia

Ancient Egypt developed along the Nile. Nubia, farther south, was home to distinct societies including the kingdom of Kush. Archaeologists study inscriptions, buildings and objects to understand these civilizations. Many questions remain about the lives of ordinary people. Myths tell us about beliefs, but are not automatically evidence of events.
