CHAPTER 1 — THE MIND OF A DEAL MAKER

A deal maker doesn’t start with money.
He starts with perception.

Where others see buildings, he sees leverage.
Where others see price, he sees pressure.

Every deal begins in silence.
Before the contract…
before the lender…
before the numbers…

It begins in thought.

The mind of a deal maker is not loud.
It is precise.

It asks questions others skip:
Who is stuck?
Who is desperate?
Who is overconfident?

Because inside those answers…
is opportunity.
